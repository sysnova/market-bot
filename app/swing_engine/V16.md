# Swing 16: retain recovery entry geometry

Adds recovery_breakout_level, recovery_intraday_rebound_low and
recovery_confirmation_bar_at to confirmed recovery AnalysisResults. Values
come from the same completed bars used by the recovery gate. The breakout is
the preceding configured range high; the rebound low is the minimum of the
recent half used for the higher-low comparison. No entry threshold, stop,
target, verdict or score changes. MarketBot 7.65.0 uses the existing 3.5.0
strategy artifact with this implementation.

These fields let downstream consumers preserve the actual confirming
geometry, rather than infer it from later Swing analyses. Full component
analyses pass through LocalAlert -> EntrySignal -> entry checkpoint.
