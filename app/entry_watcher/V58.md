# Entry Watcher 5.8: levels owned by the entry rule

MarketBot 7.62.0 selects implementation 5.8.0 and strategy artifact 1.6.0.
Assembly 7.61.0 and prior implementations remain available for replay and rollback.

AMZN's L1 on 2026-08-24 paired Intraday's stop 262.5610 with Swing's target
277.7360 at entry 263.219. This produced R/R 22.06. Intraday's own target
was 264.2061, yielding R/R 1.50. The recorded entry snapshot is a regression
fixture. Early L1's configured minimum is 1.5, not 2: the corrected AMZN
snapshot still qualifies for early L1, but not the 2R continuation gate.

For early L1 and mature Intraday-triggered Core entries, the stop and target
must both come from the same Intraday assessment: invalidation_level and
objective_level. Swing is context/approval, never a substitute target or
stop for those rules. Missing, nonfinite or incorrectly ordered levels
reject the entry; no other horizon or frozen watch stop can rescue it.
R/R is recomputed at the actual candidate entry price. Threshold comparisons
use the unrounded result. Early L1 remains 1.5R; continuation remains 2R.
Mature in-zone entries retain their existing Intraday confirmation gates.

The observed impulse-pullback rule retains its own paired levels: observed
peak as objective and observed pullback low minus its ATR buffer as stop.
Its chronological and Swing approval gates from 5.7 remain in effect.

Confirmed transitions carry the exact stop and target used for validation.
The watch stores entry_rule_levels with source, price, levels and R/R, plus
the Intraday analysis ID, version, time and setup where applicable. Reasons
identify entry_levels_source for downstream events and failure reviews.
The frozen original watch zone and invalidation remain untouched. Historical
checkpoints are not recalculated and no broker orders are generated.

This fixes level attribution, not the predictive quality of the trigger.
It does not establish that a rejected or accepted trade would be profitable.
