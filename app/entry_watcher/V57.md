# Entry Watcher 5.7: observed pullbacks

MarketBot 7.61.0 selects implementation 5.7.0 and strategy artifact 1.5.0.
The previous assembly 7.60.0 remains available for rollback.

The HOOD L1 recorded on 2026-08-19 used Swing's structural liquidity high
108.21 as its impulse peak. That produced a pullback zone 97.9203–101.8497,
stop 96.2382 and R/R 3.65 despite Swing's entry gate being false. The
committed regression fixture reproduces those exact calculations with 5.6.

Version 5.7 records prices and observation timestamps from the analysis
stream. It requires start < peak < pullback low < rising observation,
followed by the existing intraday reclaim, freshness and R/R gates.
Structural resistance is never substituted for an observed impulse peak.
These are sampled assessment prices, not a complete reconstruction of every
market-bar high or low. Duplicate/out-of-order observations cannot advance
the sequence. A new higher peak resets the pullback leg.

Both early-entry paths require explicit Swing approval: a FAVORABLE bullish
continuation with swing_entry_gate_passed, or a FAVORABLE STRUCTURE_RECOVERY
with recovery_entry_gate_passed. Recovery is not rejected just because its
original structure was damaged. Long context must remain eligible and bullish.
An extended impulse cannot bypass the pullback check through the former
second-leg fallback.

Pending legacy impulse state has no auditable peak time. The next fresh
observation restarts its sequence. Already confirmed entries retain their
state, stops and targets; frozen original watch levels are never rewritten.

Failure Lab now includes analyses stored at the exact checkpoint event and
a deduplicated timeline from supplied opportunity events. It retains entry
evidence when bounding the timeline, exposes omissions and uses observation
time as well as as_of to distinguish entry information from later evidence.

Early exits on failed acceptance remain a backtest candidate. Test only
confirmed entries, evaluate at completed bars, execute on the next available
price with costs/gaps, and compare missed recoveries as well as reduced loss.
This change does not introduce automatic early exits or broker execution.
