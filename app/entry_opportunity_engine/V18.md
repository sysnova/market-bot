# Entry Opportunity 18: consistent simulated exits

MarketBot 7.63.0 selects Entry Opportunity 18.0.0. Earlier assemblies retain
their own marking behavior through versioned hooks.

For long checkpoints and open legs, a candle that opens beyond the stop or
target fills at its open. Otherwise a touched stop/target fills at that
level, with stop first if OHLC cannot resolve both touches. The original
watch stop uses the same gap treatment. Short entries retain their existing
gap-aware implementation.

An exit candle contributes only its open and exit price to excursion
statistics. Its later extrema and close cannot be attributed to a closed
trade. Previously observed extrema remain; intrabar excursions are a
conservative bound when the exact path is unavailable.

When an analytical decision closes a checkpoint, all still-open legs of
that same entry close at the same price and time. Identity includes family,
entry time/price, stop, target and the setup ID when present. Already-closed
legs (including an Intraday session close) and unrelated strategies remain
unchanged. Existing history is never rewritten on restart.

The JPM regression uses the stored pre-exit opportunity and the 2026-09-09
opening candle: open 352, high 352.3527, low 349.5, close 350. A gap-only
replay exits at 352 rather than the unreachable 353.0933 stop. This does
not replace JPM's recorded earlier premarket analytical exit at 351.50.

Dashboard rows expose checkpoint status separately from ticker tracking.
Closed entries show their recorded exit price/time and no remaining risk
to a stop. The status filter applies to the row, including Failure Lab's
filtered universe. Historical recorded P/L remains historical.
