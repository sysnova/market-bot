# Entry Opportunity 20: failed recovery exit

Assembly 7.65.0 selects Opportunity 20.0.0 and Swing 16.0.0. Swing decisions
and entry thresholds are unchanged. Swing adds the actual breakout level,
recent-half rebound low and confirming bar timestamp to confirmed recovery
analyses. The alert-to-signal adapter now carries the complete component
analyses. Opportunity captures them on that entry's checkpoint, separately
from mutable latest analyses. It never replaces an existing entry snapshot.

## Exit rule 1.0.0

Only actual LONG Core Recovery L1-L4 entries with matching, favorable Swing
STRUCTURE_RECOVERY evidence qualify. Require the original recovery setup ID,
approved gates, positive geometry and evidence no more than 45 minutes old
and not later than entry. Freeze that entry's recovery AVWAP, breakout level,
intraday rebound low, daily reaction low, correction pivot and source ID.

Aggregate final RTH 1-minute bars into aligned 15-minute intervals. A complete
interval requires all 15 sequential minutes. Partial entry intervals, gaps,
duplicates, out-of-order bars, provisional bars and extended hours cannot
create a confirmation. A warning requires a close below BOTH frozen recovery
AVWAP and breakout level. Two consecutive complete intervals below both,
with the second close lower than the first, prepare an exit. A reclaim resets
the warning. Nonadjacent intervals, including overnight, cannot confirm it.

Execute at the next observed regular-session minute open, never at a close
before the decision existed. If the open already gaps through the original
stop, active profit protection or target, retain that price/outcome; otherwise
use RECOVERY_FAILED. A later intrabar stop touch cannot override a decision
already executed at the open. Prior original stop, target and profit exits
remain active while failure confirmation forms. Preserve original levels.
Close only matching still-open horizon legs; unrelated entries remain managed
by their own rules. These are simulated entries/exits, not broker orders.

Persist partial aggregation, warning, and pending exit state inside the
checkpoint JSON, so restarting does not forget or double-count confirmation.
NATS events use the existing transactional outbox: recovery_failure_warning,
recovery_failure_confirmation_reset, recovery_failure_exit_pending, and final
core_recovery_l2_recovery_failed / corresponding horizon reasons. The dashboard
shows recovery under review, pending exit and RECUPERACION FALLIDA distinctly.

## Compatibility and evidence limits

Contracts add default-empty EntrySignal.entry_analyses, checkpoint.entry_analyses,
optional checkpoint.recovery_exit and terminal outcome RECOVERY_FAILED. New
readers accept old snapshots. Old strict readers cannot read the expanded
payloads: deploy producers and consumers together before enabling 7.65. State
uses existing JSON persistence, with no SQL column migration.

Legacy open recoveries lacking complete entry evidence keep existing stops
and profit protection. Do not reconstruct their levels from later analyses
or retrofit historical exits. Non-recovery entries cannot acquire this exit
policy. WATCH alone is not an exit condition.
